**Priority:** High
**Components:** UI
**Labels:** registration, account, public

- Given the visitor is not signed in
- And the visitor is on the home page

## Steps

1. When the visitor opens the account menu
2. And the visitor activates "Register"

## Expected result

- Then the registration page is shown
- And the address is `/account/register`
- And the heading is "Registration"
